# Watercolor — approved production set

Status: **COMPLETE & LIVE — 6 owner-approved portrait-format infographics published on the website**

## 1. Category role

Watercolor teaches how water, transparency, paper and layered washes behave.

The sequence should feel practical and forgiving:
- water ratio and transparency
- wet paper versus dry paper
- botanical brush shapes
- soft sky washes and lifting
- repeated sky practice / mood variation
- a small complete landscape

## 2. Approved set

1. **Watercolor First Flower**
   - water ratio
   - light sketch
   - transparent first wash
   - dry glaze
   - stem and leaves

2. **Wet-on-Wet & Wet-on-Dry**
   - compare damp and dry paper
   - soft spread versus sharper edges
   - learn when to combine both

3. **Simple Leaves & Botanical Shapes**
   - simple leaf silhouettes
   - single brush shapes
   - stems, pairs and small botanical groups

4. **Soft Sky / Cloud Washes**
   - this is the **main Lesson 4**
   - its layout and visual language match Lessons 1–3 most closely
   - light sky wash, lifting, gentle color and soft edges

5. **Sky Wash Practice & Variations**
   - this is the **additional approved companion guide**
   - do not replace the main Soft Sky lesson
   - palette preparation, wet surface, wet-on-wet flow, lifting and several sky moods

6. **Small Watercolor Landscape**
   - simple scene
   - sky first
   - big land shapes
   - water / ground details
   - selected dark focal accents

## 3. Series visual identity

- portrait orientation
- warm watercolor-paper background
- visible transparent washes and soft pigment edges

## 4. Relationship between the two sky guides

Both Soft Sky images are owner-approved.

They have different roles:
- `watercolor-soft-sky-cloud-washes` = main Lesson 4 and series-consistent teaching poster
- `watercolor-sky-wash-practice` = companion/practice poster with additional sky-mood variations

They are intentionally both retained. Do not retire one merely because their subject overlaps.
