# Content production roadmap after Human Drawing Batch 01

The website is a father–daughter art journey, not a comprehensive art-history course. Every new subject is independent; no grading, logins, AI judge, subscription, commerce or public critique.

## Batch 01 — Human drawing (ready after eight artwork files arrive)
Face basics, facial expressions, head angles, faces by age; figure proportions, standing figure, sitting poses, body silhouettes.

## Batch 02 — Character styles
Four short approachable collections, each with a clear original visual guide and comparisons rather than stereotyped templates:
- Manga-inspired face and hair; simple emotion and one full character.
- Manhwa/webtoon-inspired linework, shape language, gentle digital color.
- Manhua-inspired ink/line rhythm and character clothing exploration.
- Cartoon and Western-comics shape language; expressions and silhouettes.
A character's anatomy is allowed to change by art style, but body balance and visual storytelling must still work.

## Batch 03 — Pencil art
A proper grayscale portrait: face planes, light/shadow families and hair groups. Pencil scenery: mountain/forest, a quiet street, water reflections, distance through contrast and perspective. Each uses a consistent scene/face across five visual stages. No need for photo-realistic digital painting when a pencil study is the actual lesson.

## Batch 04 — Color & drawing apps
Simple watercolor wash; where to leave white paper; glazing and wet-on-wet differences. Digital skin color with only a few layers: base, shadow, warmth, light; before/after comparisons. Avoid suggesting that more layers automatically mean better art.

## Review gate for every batch
Artwork continuity, credible construction, readable text, honest rights/credits, WebP image budget, complete links, desktop/iPad/mobile viewport checks, tests/build/deployment. Publish in batches without per-image approval once the approved style is followed; hold back a specific image if it fails its own checks.
