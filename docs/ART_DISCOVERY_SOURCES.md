# Explore Art editorial and source notes (v0.2)

## Purpose
The website remains a family art journey, not an art-history syllabus. Browse through materials, a handful of widely recognizable artistic traditions, and approachable artist infographics. No grading or account required.

## Content model
- **Materials & tools:** graphite, colored pencil, watercolor, oil, acrylic, crayon/pastel, ink/wash, Vietnamese lacquer, digital drawing. A medium is not the same as an artistic style.
- **Ways of seeing:** Renaissance, Impressionism, Post-Impressionism, Cubism, and Chinese ink/wash. These are a teaching selection, not a complete taxonomy: Chinese ink painting is a long-standing tradition rather than a Western-style movement.
- **Artists:** Leonardo, Monet, Van Gogh, Fan Kuan have short text infographics, a named work with a museum link and an optional drawing prompt. Michelangelo, Picasso, Berthe Morisot and Bùi Xuân Phái are shorter external-reference cards.

## Image permissions and provenance
Only the featured Van Gogh painting is displayed by reference to a confirmed public-domain Metropolitan Museum image. Other artworks are linked to official museum pages. The remaining imagery in cards and infographics is original generic SVG website illustration, **not** an original work by the named artist. Images in public/showcase/ will be replaced with curated licensed art in future rounds. Avoid copying museum images when image download/reuse rights are not confirmed. Always keep visible credits and source URLs.

## Visual themes
- **Studio:** neutral paper, graphite and sage.
- **Playful:** sophisticated lilac, blush, peach and mint; deliberately suited to a teen rather than a small child.
- Switch stored locally in browser localStorage as `g-art-theme`. No user account or server profile.
- Header uses a G-Art-specific colorway of the approved G/open-book/star symbol. Original G Learning brand SVG remains untouched.
