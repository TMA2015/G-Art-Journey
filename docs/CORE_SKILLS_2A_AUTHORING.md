# Core Drawing Skills 2A — authoring packet

This is the first production unit after Character Styles. It is intentionally small: four transferable skills, not another large curriculum.

## Production order
1. Hand structure — highest QA value because hand errors were the most common failure in the previous batch.
2. Eye structure — establishes a reliable feature-construction standard.
3. Hair masses and flow — teaches grouping and silhouette rather than strand copying.
4. Fabric from tension and gravity — connects anatomy, gesture and clothing.

## Visual direction
- Graphite-first drawings: neutral pencil/ink values, paper texture and clean construction lines.
- G-Art pink/peach/mint/purple is used only for titles, arrows, step numbers and small callout shapes.
- The layout may change per lesson. Hands can use a large anatomy plate; hair may use one hero head plus variants; fabric may use four cause-based mini studies.
- Do not make all models the same girl. Different examples should have different faces, hair, ages or clothing where the skill allows.

## Non-negotiable human QA
- correct anatomy / anatomically correct
- exactly five fingers when a whole hand is visible
- detailed hands, not tiny ambiguous blobs
- face/eyes aligned to the chosen head angle
- natural expression
- proportionate body anatomy
- clear full-length or medium framing when body landmarks are being taught
- same subject/angle throughout cumulative steps unless viewpoint change is the lesson
