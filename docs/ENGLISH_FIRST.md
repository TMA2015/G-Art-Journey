# English-first content policy (v0.5)

G-Art Journey is a calm art space, not a school course. English is the only public language for now.

## Live routes
- Canonical root `/G-Art-Journey/` and its detail pages are English.
- Existing `/G-Art-Journey/en/` URLs remain as English legacy aliases; their canonical link points to the root equivalent. Internal links use root paths.
- Do not expose a VN/EN switch while the Vietnamese edition is incomplete.
- The existing `src/i18n` dictionaries, metadata and Vietnamese SVG assets remain available as groundwork, but new content only needs editorial-quality English at this stage.

## Plain-language writing rules
- Write for a curious beginner (including a young teen) without patronizing the reader.
- Introduce an object or action in familiar words before technical labels: “light areas and dark areas” before “values”, “shadow on the paper” before “cast shadow”, and “the middle guide line” before “centerline”.
- When a term helps further exploration, explain it in one sentence and reuse it consistently. Keep headings short and avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Separate art movements, media, and illustration styles; do not present traditions as one-size-fits-all techniques.
- No grading, ranks, AI assessment or sales.
- Image titles/captions and downloadable infographics must match visible page language. Proper names and original artwork titles may remain as originally titled.
- Preserve authorship and source attribution. Gallery captions belong to the artists and must never be automatically rewritten.

## Future Vietnamese rollout
Create complete Vietnamese content and posters as a separate, reviewed `/vi/` edition. Re-enable a language switch only when all linked pages, images, menus, and internal navigation have equivalent approved content. Do not make root and `/en/` depend on untranslated Vietnamese data.

## Acceptance
Test the English root and legacy alias, home slider, all 21 content route pairs, responsive header, both Studio/Playful themes, poster downloads, gallery and quick section navigation. CI checks content/asset build; owner checks device visuals before release.
