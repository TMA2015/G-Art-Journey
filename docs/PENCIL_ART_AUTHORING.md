## Category role

Pencil Art teaches the medium itself: line control, pressure, value, form, objects, texture and portrait rendering.

Approved progression:
1. **Pencil Control — Lines, Pressure & Marks**
2. **Shade Simple Forms**
3. **Draw Everyday Objects**
4. **Texture with Graphite**
5. **A Pencil Portrait**
6. **Facial Features in Pencil** — portrait add-on, not a replacement

## Lesson distinction

### 1. Pencil Control
Control pressure, line weight, hatching and mark direction.

### 2. Shade Simple Forms
Understand light and shadow on sphere, cube, cylinder and cone.

### 3. Everyday Objects
Find simple forms inside common objects before proportion and shading.

### 4. Texture with Graphite
Use different marks and edge behavior to suggest material surfaces.

### 5. Pencil Portrait
Combine head construction, feature placement, value grouping and selective finish.

### 6. Facial Features in Pencil
An add-on for portrait study. Eyes and mouth receive the most space; nose and ear receive smaller dedicated studies. It does not replace Lesson 5.
