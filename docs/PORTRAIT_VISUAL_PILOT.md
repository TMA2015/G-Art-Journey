# Portrait visual sequence pilot

This pilot adds one original SVG with five consecutive studies of the **same invented face**, at the same angle and proportion:

1. Find the overall head shape and center guide.
2. Place the eyes, nose and mouth.
3. Group hair and add shoulders.
4. Add light, middle and dark areas.
5. Sharpen selected details and step back.

It is intentionally **an educational graphite-style vector study**, not a photograph, a historical artwork, or a finished natural-media pencil drawing. It remains small and sharp when enlarged; no paid hosting is needed. Its download and source are on the pencil portrait guide.

Review on desktop and iPad: same facial landmarks in each panel, enough whitespace for captions, clear five-stage progression, no clipping of the full head/shoulders and easy horizontal scrolling on a narrow screen. Check both Studio and Playful themes. Replace or refine after editorial/art review; do not expand the pattern to all guides before the first pilot is accepted.
